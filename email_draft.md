**Subject:** [Weekly Pulse] Groww App Sentiment: UI Still Strong, but Technical Stability and Transparency Risks Increasing

Hi Product Manager,

I've just finalized the review analysis for this week based on **1,462 new reviews** (Avg Rating: **3.65 ⭐**). While our onboarding and UI simplicity continue to receive high praise, we are seeing a significant uptick in friction from our active user base.

### 🔝 Top 3 Themes
1.  **Investment Experience (30%)**: Users praise the app's convenience and "one-stop shop" feel.
2.  **App Performance (23%)**: Increasing reports of technical glitches, freezing, and chart lags.
3.  **Brokerage Charges (12%)**: Dissatisfaction with perceived hidden fees and high transaction costs.

### 💬 Real User Quotes
*   "excellent, in terms of convenience, easiness and transparency. I find it (GrowW App) as one shop solution for all my investment needs."
*   "facing technical glitches while exiting when every second is important kindly correct it immediately..."
*   "worst part of groww is they cut more charges than what they said... it feels like a scam, i sell a stock and charges should be between 7 to 8 but they cut 30rs"

### 🚀 Suggested Product Actions
1.  **High Priority: Chart Drawing Sync.** Active traders are frustrated that levels drawn on one device don't appear on others. This is an immediate retention risk.
2.  **Transparency: Interactive Charge Breakdown.** Add a "View Estimated Fees" toggle before trade placement that clearly separates Groww brokerage from regulatory taxes.
3.  **Reliability: Peak-Hour Load Prep.** Performance audits are needed for the 9:15 AM and 3:20 PM windows, where most "app hang" reports are concentrated.
