# Groww Weekly Pulse
*A snapshot of user sentiment and actionable insights from recent app reviews.*

## 🎯 Top 3 Themes
1.  **Investment Experience**: Users largely praise the Groww app for its ease of use, transparency, and suitability for both beginners and experienced investors, often calling it a "one-stop solution."
2.  **App Performance**: A significant number of users are experiencing critical technical glitches, slow loading times, app freezing, and delays in portfolio/SIP updates, severely impacting their trading and investment activities.
3.  **Brokerage Charges**: Many users express strong dissatisfaction with what they perceive as high, unexplained, or hidden brokerage and external charges, leading to distrust and comparisons with competitor pricing.

## 🚀 Action Ideas
1.  **Enhance App Stability & Speed**: The Engineering and Product teams should immediately prioritize investigation and resolution of critical performance issues like app freezing, slow loading, and delayed data updates, particularly during peak trading times and for MF portfolio tracking.
2.  **Boost Brokerage Transparency**: The Product and Growth teams need to design and implement a clearer, itemized breakdown of all transaction costs, including brokerage, taxes, and other external charges, on trade execution screens and in statements to rebuild user trust and address perceptions of hidden fees.
3.  **Refine Feature Set & User Experience**: The Product and UX/UI teams should evaluate requests for strategic advanced features (e.g., Trailing Stop Loss, SWP) while also assessing feedback on app "bloat" (e.g., loans/UPI tabs) to ensure the core investment journey remains clean, focused, and user-friendly.
